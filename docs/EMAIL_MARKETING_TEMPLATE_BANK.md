# Email Marketing Template Bank

Use these 16 starter templates for the Admin Email Marketing module. Replace links/placeholders where needed before production use.

Common placeholders:
- `{{student_name}}`
- `{{cohort_name}}`
- `{{program_name}}`
- `{{lms_link}}`
- `{{resource_title}}`
- `{{resource_link}}`
- `{{community_link}}`
- `{{project_link}}`
- `{{support_email}}`
- `{{deadline_date}}`

## 1. New Resource Added

Campaign key: auto-generated from title.

Subject: `New resource added for {{cohort_name}}`

Preview text: `A useful learning resource is ready for your current module.`

Body:

```text
Hi {{student_name}},

We have added a useful resource for {{cohort_name}}: {{resource_title}}.

This resource is meant to help you revise the current topic, strengthen your understanding, and move ahead with more confidence.

You can access it here:
{{resource_link}}

Please go through it today or keep it saved for your next study slot.

Regards,
Skilled Sapiens Team
```
