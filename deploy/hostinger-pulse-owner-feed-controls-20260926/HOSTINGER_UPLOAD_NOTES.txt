Pulse Hostinger package - owner feed controls

What changed:
- Post owners now see Edit on their own Pulse feed posts.
- Post owners now see Hide on their own posts. This archives the post and removes it from the feed instead of hard deleting it.
- Comment owners now see Edit on their own comments.
- Comment owners now see Delete on their own comments.
- Owner controls also work for anonymous posts/comments because ownership is checked by profile id, not display name.
- Includes the earlier Pulse content updates and iPad/tablet navigation/header fixes.
